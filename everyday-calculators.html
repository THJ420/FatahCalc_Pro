Everyday Utility Calculators guide

Some calculations are too small for a spreadsheet but still worth getting right. These tools keep the interface short: enter the bill or birth date, adjust the options and read the result.

Both run entirely in your browser, so the numbers you enter stay on your device.

Everyday Utility FAQ

Can I split a bill between more than two people?

Yes. Enter any number of people and the tip calculator divides the total, including gratuity, evenly.

How accurate is the age-in-days calculator?

It uses actual calendar dates, so days, weeks, months and hours are accurate. The heartbeat figure is an estimate based on an average resting heart rate.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
