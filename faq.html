Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about pricing, offline use, privacy, accuracy and how FatahCalc Pro is built.

Getting started

What is FatahCalc Pro?

FatahCalc Pro is a free online calculator and converter suite by FatahTech. It includes 52 tools across 12 categories, all running in your browser with live results and no account.

Is FatahCalc Pro completely free to use?

Yes. Every calculator and converter is free, with no paywall, trial, account or advertising. There are no usage limits and nothing to unlock.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There is no sign-up or login. Open a tool and start typing. Favorites and history are stored locally in your browser.

How do I find a specific calculator?

Use the search box in the header, or press / or Ctrl/Cmd + K. Search matches tool names, descriptions and keywords. You can also browse the calculators and converters hubs by category.

Offline and installation

Can I install FatahCalc Pro as an offline PWA app?

Yes. Most browsers offer Install or Add to Home Screen. A service worker caches the app shell and tools you have opened, so they keep working without a connection. Currency rates require a network connection to refresh.

Which tools work offline?

The app shell and any tool you have opened are cached. Tools you have never visited need a connection the first time. Previously fetched currency rates can be used offline and are marked as cached.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. The layout is mobile-first, with a collapsible navigation drawer and converter fields that stack vertically on narrow screens. Every tool is usable with touch and a keyboard.

Privacy and data

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs in your browser. There is no analytics, advertising or account system, so inputs, favorites and history stay on your device. The only network request is the currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.

What data is stored on my device?

Your theme choice, favorites, recent tools, calculation history and any custom units. These use browser local storage and can be removed by clearing site data. Nothing is uploaded.

Are there ads or trackers?

No. FatahCalc Pro has no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking. The external resources are limited to web fonts and an icon font loaded from public CDNs.

Accuracy and methods

How accurate are the results?

The tools use standard formulas and keep full precision internally while rounding the displayed value. Conversions use exact factors where an exact definition exists, such as 1 inch = 25.4 mm.

Where do currency exchange rates come from?

From a free public exchange-rate feed, cached locally. They are reference mid-market rates, not transaction rates. Banks and card issuers add spreads and fees, so the final amount will differ.

Which grading scale is used?

The GPA calculator uses the Bangladesh/UGC 4.00 scale. The grade calculator also offers US letter grades with and without plus/minus, UK honours and the US 4.0 mapping. Always check your institution’s official scale.

Can I rely on these tools for financial or health decisions?

Treat them as well-implemented arithmetic, not professional advice. Loan figures will not match a lender’s schedule once fees and day-count rules apply, and health formulas are population-level estimates. Confirm consequential decisions with a qualified professional.

Can I use the cipher tool to protect sensitive information?

No. The cipher tool implements classical ciphers such as Caesar, ROT13, Atbash, Vigenère and A1Z26. They are for puzzles and learning, not security. Use modern encryption for anything confidential.

Technical

What is FatahCalc Pro built with?

Semantic HTML5, CSS custom properties, and vanilla ES6+ JavaScript with ES modules. There is no framework and no bundler. Routing is a small custom router, and each tool loads on demand.

I found a bug or want a new tool. Where do I report it?

Contact FatahTech through the main website. Include the tool name, the values you entered and what you expected instead; that makes the report much easier to reproduce.
