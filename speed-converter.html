How the Speed Converter works

Every speed unit on this page is stored as a single number: how many metres per second it represents. Converting is then always the same two-step move, no matter which of the {n{'}'} directions you pick:

value_in_base = input × factor(from) result = value_in_base ÷ factor(to)

That is why FatahCalc Pro needs one speed page instead of one page per pair — the dropdowns simply choose which two factors get plugged into the same formula. Results are rounded to 10 significant digits and shown in scientific notation when they get extreme.

Reference factors

  • 1 km/h = 1000/3600 = 0.2777… m/s
  • 1 mph = 0.44704 m/s exactly (1,609.344 m ÷ 3,600 s)
  • 1 knot = 1 nautical mile per hour = 0.514444 m/s
  • Mach 1 ≈ 340.29 m/s at sea level and 15 °C — it falls with temperature, so this is an approximation

Pace is the odd one out

Minutes per kilometre is an inverse measure: the faster you go, the smaller the number. It cannot be a simple multiplier, so it supplies its own conversion closures (1000 / (pace × 60)) exactly like the temperature scales do. Handy mental anchors: a 6:00 min/km pace is 10 km/h, and 5:00 min/km is 12 km/h.

Speed Converter FAQ

How fast is one knot?

One knot is one nautical mile per hour, about 1.852 km/h or 1.151 mph.

What is mach 1?

Mach 1 is the speed of sound in the surrounding medium, roughly 1,235 km/h at sea level. The converter uses a standard sea-level reference.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
