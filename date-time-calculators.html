Date & Time Calculators guide

Date arithmetic is deceptively error-prone: months have different lengths, leap years add a day and “one month” is not a fixed number of days. These tools compare calendar dates properly instead of dividing a day count by 30 or 365.

Use them for age checks, project deadlines, notice periods, timesheets and live countdowns. Each result is calculated in your timezone and never uploaded.

Date & Time FAQ

Do the date calculators account for leap years?

Yes. Age and date-difference calculations compare actual calendar dates, so February 29 and month-length differences are handled correctly.

How are business days calculated?

Weekends are excluded from the business-day count. Public holidays are not assumed because they vary by country and year.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
