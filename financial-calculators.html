Financial & Business Calculators guide

Financial calculations are easier to trust when the assumptions are visible. These tools separate principal, rate, term and fees, and they show the total cost rather than only the monthly figure.

Use them to compare loan offers, estimate compound growth, work out sale prices, add or remove VAT, and check margin before setting a price. They are estimating tools, not a substitute for a lender’s quote or professional advice.

Financial FAQ

Can I use these calculators for a real loan or mortgage?

They use standard amortization and interest formulas, so they are a good estimate. Lenders add fees, insurance and their own rounding rules, so confirm the final schedule with the lender.

Do the financial tools include tax and fees?

The VAT/sales tax tool handles tax explicitly. The loan and interest calculators do not assume fees or tax, so add those separately if they apply.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
