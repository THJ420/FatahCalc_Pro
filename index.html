Frequently asked questions

Is FatahCalc Pro completely free to use?

Yes. All 52 calculators and converters are free, with no paywall, trial, account or advertising. There is nothing to unlock and no usage limit.

Can I install FatahCalc Pro as an offline PWA app?

Yes. Use your browser’s Install or Add to Home Screen option. A service worker caches the app shell and the tools you have opened, so they keep working offline. Fresh currency rates require a connection.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. The math runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no ads and no account system, so your inputs, favorites and history stay on your device. The only network request is the currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.

How many calculators and converters are included?

There are 52 tools across 12 categories: 31 calculators for math, finance, health, dates, grades, electrical work and everyday use, plus 21 converters for units, number systems, color, electrical values and currency.

Do I need an account or sign-up?

No. Open a tool and start typing. Favorites and history are stored in this browser only, so they are private to the device and do not require a login.

How accurate are the results?

The tools use standard formulas and keep full precision internally while rounding the displayed value. They are calculation aids, not professional financial, medical or electrical advice; confirm consequential decisions with a qualified professional.
