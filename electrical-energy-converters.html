Electrical & Energy Converters guide

Electrical work spans many orders of magnitude, from microvolts to megavolts and from hertz to terahertz. These converters use SI prefixes and standard factors so a value can move between scales without manual arithmetic.

Each tool lists every supported unit and updates the others as you type. Use them for electronics, radio, testing and energy comparisons.

Electrical & Energy FAQ

Do these converters use SI prefixes correctly?

Yes. The voltage, frequency, power and energy tools use the standard SI relationships, including the distinction between decimal and binary units where relevant.

What is dBm used for?

dBm expresses power relative to one milliwatt on a logarithmic scale. It is common in radio, Wi-Fi and other communication systems.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
