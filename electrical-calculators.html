Electrical & Physics Calculators guide

Electrical calculations become much easier when voltage, current, resistance and power are treated as one connected system. These tools solve the standard relationships in both directions and show the formula used.

They are intended for study, prototyping and quick checks. For mains wiring or anything safety-critical, follow local electrical codes and consult a licensed electrician.

Electrical & Physics FAQ

Which formulas do the electrical calculators use?

Ohm’s law (V = I × R), the power equations (P = V × I, P = I²R and P = V²/R) and their rearrangements. The power-cost tool also converts watts and hours into kilowatt-hours.

Are these electrical calculators safe for mains wiring?

They are calculation aids, not a substitute for electrical codes, testing or a licensed electrician. Do not rely on them for safety-critical installation decisions.

Are my calculations private?
