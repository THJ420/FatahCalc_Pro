Unit & Measurement Converters guide

A converter should not make you choose a direction before you start. Each tool here has two unit menus, a swap button, and live conversion in either field — so cm to inches and inches to cm use the same page.

Factors are stored once and applied consistently, with exact definitions used where they exist (one inch is exactly 25.4 mm). Results are displayed to a sensible precision and cached for offline use after the first visit.

Everyday Units FAQ

Are the unit conversion factors exact?

Where an exact definition exists, yes — for example, one inch is defined as exactly 25.4 mm. Other factors are standard physical constants or conventional values and are stated in the tool.

Why is there one converter per quantity instead of one page per direction?

One page with two dropdowns replaces dozens of one-way pages. It keeps the interface short, avoids broken bookmarks and makes swapping direction instant.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
