Converter FAQ

How many free converters are included?

There are 21 converters across five categories: everyday units, number systems and encoding, color, electrical and energy, and currency.

Do the converters work offline?

Yes after the first visit. The app shell and opened tools are cached locally. Currency rates use the most recently cached table until a connection is available.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
