Math FAQ

Are these math calculators free to use?

Yes. All 11 calculators are free, with no ads, paywall or sign-up. They run entirely in your browser.

Do the math calculators show their working?

The standard deviation, LCM/GCF and quadratic tools show intermediate steps, and every tool includes a “How this tool works” explanation with the relevant formulas.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
