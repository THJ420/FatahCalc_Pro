Number System & Encoding Converters guide

Number and encoding conversions are easy to get wrong when a leading zero, case or separator changes the meaning. These tools validate input and show several representations side by side so you can compare them.

They are aimed at programmers, students, radio hobbyists and puzzle fans. Classical ciphers are included for learning and games — they are not security tools.

Number Systems & Encoding FAQ

What is the difference between ASCII and Unicode?

ASCII defines 128 basic English characters and control codes. Unicode covers most of the world’s writing systems; these tools focus on the ASCII range and common encodings.

Can I use the cipher converter for private data?

No. Caesar, ROT13, Atbash, Vigenère and A1Z26 are classical ciphers and are easy to break. Use modern encryption for anything confidential.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
