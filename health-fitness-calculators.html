Health & Fitness Calculators guide

Health numbers are useful context, not a diagnosis. These calculators use established formulas — BMI categories, Mifflin–St Jeor for BMR and four common ideal-weight equations — and present a range where a single number would be misleading.

Everything runs locally. Enter height, weight, age and activity level to explore how the estimates change; nothing is sent to a server.

Health & Fitness FAQ

Are these health calculators medical advice?

No. They are arithmetic tools based on population-level formulas. They do not account for body composition, medical conditions or clinical context. Speak to a qualified professional for health decisions.

Do the health calculators support metric and imperial units?

The BMI calculator supports both metric and imperial input. The BMR and ideal-weight tools state their unit options on the page; check before entering values.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
