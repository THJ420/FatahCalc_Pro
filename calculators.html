Calculator FAQ

How many free calculators are included?

There are 31 calculators across seven categories: math, financial, health and fitness, date and time, grades and education, electrical and physics, and everyday utility.

Do I need to install anything to use the calculators?

No. They run directly in your browser. You can optionally install FatahCalc Pro as a PWA for offline access and a home-screen icon.

Are my calculations private?

Yes. Every calculation runs locally in your browser. FatahCalc Pro has no analytics, no advertising and no account system, so your inputs and history stay on this device. The only network request is the live currency-rate feed, which contains no personal data.
